Bedtime Procrastination Scale (BPS)

What is bedtime procrastination?

Bedtime procrastination refers to going to bed later than intended, despite having no external reason preventing you from doing so.

Understanding your BPS score
The Bedtime Procrastination Scale contains nine statements. Each response is scored from 1 to 5. Your final score is the average of your nine corrected responses.

Range: 1–5
1 = Lower score
5 = Higher score

A higher score indicates a greater tendency to postpone bedtime. There are no universally established clinical cut-offs for low, moderate or high severity. This is a self-report measure, not a diagnosis.
